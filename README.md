# AI-Assisted Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales and profitability data for 2024 and 2025 using Microsoft Excel, PivotTables, data modeling, and AI-assisted business analysis.

The objective is to identify sales trends, profitability patterns, regional/category performance, and major changes between 2024 and 2025.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Data Modeling
- Excel Dashboard
- Data Analysis
- AI-assisted Business Analysis

## Key Business Questions

1. Why did sales decline in 2025 compared with 2024?
2. Which category contributes the most to total sales?
3. Which category has high sales but comparatively low profit?
4. Which region performs strongest in sales and profit?
5. Which products have the highest sales and profit margins?
6. How does Online vs Store sales contribution differ?
7. Which regions, categories, or products experienced the most significant change from 2024 to 2025?

## Analysis Approach

The analysis follows a structured approach:

1. Data preparation and cleaning
2. Data modeling
3. PivotTable analysis
4. Dashboard creation
5. Identification of significant changes
6. AI-assisted investigation of potential drivers
7. Validation of observations using the available data

AI was used as an analytical assistant, not as a replacement for business judgment.

## Key Insights

- Total sales declined by approximately 3.26% in 2025 compared with 2024.
- Electronics was one of the strongest sales-contributing categories.
- West was the strongest region in overall sales.
- Product-level and regional analysis was used to investigate the major contributors to the sales change.
- The analysis distinguishes between observed facts and possible explanations rather than assuming causation.
